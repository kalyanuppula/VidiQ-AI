# VidiQ AI
### Multimodal Video Intelligence & Temporal Retrieval Platform

VidiQ AI is an Enterprise AI platform designed to make training
and operational videos searchable and useful through multimodal
understanding, timestamp-aware retrieval, and grounded question answering.

## Problem Statement

Organizations store valuable operational knowledge in videos.
Finding a specific instruction, event, or procedure inside long
videos is time-consuming.

VidiQ AI aims to solve this by enabling users to search video
content using natural language and retrieve relevant moments
with timestamp-based evidence.

## Target Use Cases

- Enterprise employee training
- Workplace safety procedures
- Machine operation guidance
- Operational knowledge retrieval
- Quality and process inspection
